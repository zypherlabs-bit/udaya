🏛️ Founding Principles

  • No Premine — 100% of UDYA is mined through Proof-of-Work. No tokens were created before the genesis block.
  • No ICO — No pre-sale, no private allocation, no insider discounts. Everyone has equal access.
  • No Founder Reward — Founders mine on equal footing with the community. No special mining rewards.
  • Fair Launch — Anyone can mine from block 1. No head start for insiders.
  • Open Source — Complete source code publicly available on GitHub. MIT-licensed.

🔐 Allocation Summary

Mining Rewards — 97.62%

20,500,000 UDYA are distributed to miners over ~120 years via the halving schedule. This ensures provable scarcity and fair distribution identical to Bitcoin's monetary policy.

Founder Allocation — 2.38%

500,000 UDYA with a 6-year linear vesting schedule and 1-year cliff. This is one of the smallest founder allocations in cryptocurrency, demonstrating commitment to fairness.

🛡️ Tier 1 — Cold Storage
  • 3-of-5 geographically distributed multisig
  • Hardware wallets (Coldcard/Ledger)
  • Air-gapped signing
  • 48-hour timelock on >10K UDYA
  • Quarterly third-party audits
🔐 Tier 2 — Operational
  • 2-of-3 to 4-of-6 multisig
  • PSBT workflow required
  • Daily transaction limits
  • Monthly reconciliation
  • 24/7 monitoring & alerting
⚡ Tier 3 — Daily
  • 1-of-2 multisig
  • Automated top-up from parent
  • <100 UDYA per transaction
  • Real-time balance alerts
  • Monthly expense reporting
