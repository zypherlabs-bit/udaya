🗳️ Voting Process

Step-by-Step Proposal Lifecycle

  • 1. Drafting — Proposal author prepares the specification including title, description, motivation, technical details (if applicable), and voting options (For/Against/Abstain).
  • 2. Community Discussion — Pre-proposal discussion on community forums and governance channels. Minimum 7-day discussion period recommended before formal submission.
  • 3. Submission — Proposal is submitted on-chain with a 100 UDYA deposit. The proposer must hold at least 0.1% of circulating supply.
  • 4. Voting Period — Voting opens immediately upon submission and runs for 20,160 blocks (~14 days). UDYA holders vote by sending a special governance transaction.
  • 5. Tallying — Votes are tallied in real-time on-chain. Any participant can independently verify the count by analyzing the blockchain.
  • 6. Outcome — If quorum (10% participation) and majority (51% approval) are met, the proposal passes. The deposit is returned to the proposer.
  • 7. Execution — Technical upgrades are queued for activation at a specified block height. Treasury allocations are executed by the multi-sig foundation wallet. General proposals are implemented by the foundation.
  • 8. Reporting — Execution results are published on the trust portal within 7 days of proposal passing.

🏛️ Treasury Management

Udaya has no premine or foundation allocation. The treasury is funded entirely through community donations and must be managed transparently under governance oversight.

Multi-Sig Structure

RoleKey Holder
Signer 1Foundation Executive Director
Signer 2Lead Developer
Signer 3Community Representative (Elected)
Signer 4Independent Auditor
Signer 5Governance Committee Lead

Threshold: 3 of 5 signatures required for any treasury transaction

Treasury Principles

  • Transparency: All treasury transactions are published on-chain and visible on the trust portal
  • Governance Approval: All expenditures above 1,000 UDYA require a governance vote
  • Auditability: Quarterly third-party audits of treasury accounts
  • No Personal Benefit: Treasury funds cannot be used for founder or team member compensation
  • Donation Only: Treasury is funded exclusively through voluntary community donations

🛡️ Governance Principles

🎯 Progressive Decentralization

Governance powers transition from the foundation to the community over time. Initially, the foundation holds emergency powers which are progressively transferred to on-chain governance as the community matures.

🔒 Immutable Core

The 21 million supply cap, SHA-256d consensus, and halving schedule cannot be changed by governance. These parameters are hard-coded and cannot be altered by any vote.

📊 Verifiable Outcomes

All voting records are permanently stored on the blockchain. Anyone can independently verify vote counts, quorum achievement, and proposal outcomes without trusting a third party.

⚖️ Sybil Resistance

Vote weight is proportional to UDYA holdings, providing natural Sybil resistance. The proposal threshold and deposit requirement prevent spam while remaining accessible to active community members.
