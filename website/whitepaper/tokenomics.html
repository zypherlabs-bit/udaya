📊 Supply Schedule

Udaya follows Bitcoin's halving schedule exactly: block rewards are cut in half every 210,000 blocks (~4 years). The initial block reward is 50 UDYA, and after 64 halvings, the total supply reaches its hard cap of 21,000,000 UDYA.

🏛️ Economic Principles

🔒 Predictable Supply

The entire emission schedule is deterministic and verifiable. Anyone can independently calculate the supply at any future block height. There is no monetary policy discretion — the code enforces the rules.

⚖️ Fair Distribution

Without premine, ICO, or founder allocation, every participant enters on equal footing. Early miners are rewarded for securing the network, not for privileged access.

📉 Decreasing Inflation

Inflation decreases by 50% every 4 years, asymptotically approaching zero. This creates a predictable transition from issuance-based security to fee-based security over the long term.

🛡️ No Governance Over Inflation

Unlike proof-of-stake systems where validators can vote to change inflation parameters, Udaya's monetary policy is immutable and hard-coded. Participants know exactly what the supply will be decades in advance.
