📜 Rules

  • Vulnerabilities must be previously undisclosed
  • Submit detailed reports to security@Udaya.net
  • Use our PGP key for encrypted reports
  • Allow 90 days for remediation before disclosure
  • No testing on mainnet without explicit permission
  • No social engineering or physical attacks
  • No DoS attacks on production infrastructure
