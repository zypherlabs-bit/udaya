# Udaya Economic Viability Report

## Why Should Users Acquire UDYA?

### Executive Summary

Udaya (UDYA) is a next-generation Proof-of-Work cryptocurrency designed for real-world economic use. This document articulates the value proposition for all ecosystem participants.

---

## 1. Core Use Cases

### 1.1 Digital Currency
- **Peer-to-peer payments**: Send UDYA globally with minimal fees
- **Merchant payments**: Low transaction fees (fractions of a cent)
- **Cross-border transfers**: No intermediaries, no delays
- **Micropayments**: Feasible due to low fee structure

### 1.2 Store of Value
- **Fixed supply**: 21 million UDYA maximum (Bitcoin-inspired)
- **Proof-of-Work security**: Energy-backed value
- **Halving schedule**: Predictable diminishing supply
- **Fully decentralized**: No central authority

### 1.3 Network Fees
- **Transaction fees**: Paid in UDYA
- **Smart contract platform**: Future feature
- **Governance participation**: Voting requires UDYA stake

### 1.4 Mining Rewards
- **Block rewards**: 50 UDYA initially, halving every 210,000 blocks
- **Transaction fees**: Collected by miners
- **Mining incentives**: Long-term profitability
