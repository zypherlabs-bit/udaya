# Udaya Liquidity Readiness & Roadmap

## Executive Summary

This document outlines the liquidity strategy for Udaya (UDYA), including market maker requirements, treasury allocation, listing preparation, and exchange onboarding.

## 2. Market Maker Requirements

### Market Maker Selection Criteria
- Minimum 2 years of crypto market making experience
- Proven track record on 5+ exchanges
- Regulatory compliance (KYC/AML)
- Technical infrastructure for automated trading
- Weekly liquidity reports

### Market Maker Agreement Terms
| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Minimum Quote Size | $5,000 per side |
| Maximum Spread | 1% on top-tier pairs |
| Minimum Uptime | 99.5% |
| Monthly Volume Commitment | $1M+ |
| Reporting Frequency | Weekly |
| Lock-up Period | 6 months minimum |

### Incentive Structure
- **Tier 1** (Primary MM): 0.05% monthly rebate on volume
- **Tier 2** (Secondary MM): 0.03% monthly rebate on volume
- **Bonus**: Additional 0.02% for spread <0.5%

## 8. Risk Management

### Risks
| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Insufficient liquidity | High | Multiple MMs, staggered onboarding |
| Market manipulation | Medium | Monitoring, circuit breakers |
| Exchange hacks | High | Diversify across exchanges |
| Regulatory changes | Medium | Legal counsel, jurisdiction diversification |
| MM withdrawal | Medium | Backup MM agreements |

### Monitoring Dashboard Metrics
- Bid-ask spread on each exchange
- Order book depth (at 1%, 2%, 5% levels)
- 24h trading volume by pair
- Price deviation across exchanges
- Slippage simulation for $10K orders
- Market maker performance score
