# Udaya Foundation Charter

## Preamble

The Udaya Foundation (hereinafter "the Foundation") is established as a non-profit, community-governed organization dedicated to the development, promotion, and stewardship of the Udaya (UDYA) blockchain ecosystem. The Foundation operates transparently, neutrally, and in the best interests of the Udaya network, its users, developers, miners, and stakeholders.

## Article I: Mission & Purpose

### Section 1.1 Mission
The mission of the Udaya Foundation is to advance Udaya as infrastructure-grade decentralized financial infrastructure—a secure, scalable, and trust-minimized Proof-of-Work blockchain that serves global commerce.

### Section 1.2 Core Purposes
1. Maintain and improve the Udaya core protocol
2. Promote decentralization and network resilience
3. Support developer ecosystem and community growth
4. Ensure transparent governance and treasury management
5. Foster enterprise adoption and exchange integration
6. Conduct security research and adversarial testing
7. Educate the public on Proof-of-Work and decentralized systems

## Article II: Organizational Structure

### Section 2.1 Governance Bodies
- **Foundation Council**: 7 elected members serving 2-year terms
- **Technical Steering Committee**: Core developers and maintainers
- **Community Advisory Board**: Representatives from mining, exchange, and application sectors
- **Treasury Committee**: 3 members overseeing fund allocation

### Section 2.2 Election Process
Council elections occur annually via on-chain voting. Any UDYA holder with ≥0.1% of circulating supply may nominate candidates. Voting power is proportional to stake with a maximum cap of 10% per voter to prevent plutocracy.

## Article III: Treasury Management

### Section 3.1 Treasury Sources
- Block subsidy allocation (2% of block reward)
- Donations and grants
- Partnership contributions
- Service fees

### Section 3.2 Treasury Usage
Funds are allocated to:
1. Protocol development grants (40%)
2. Security audits and bug bounties (25%)
3. Community and ecosystem growth (20%)
4. Operational expenses (10%)
5. Reserve fund (5%)

## Article IV: Amendment Process

Amendments to this charter require:
1. Proposal submission with 30-day comment period
2. 60% approval from Foundation Council
3. On-chain ratification vote with ≥15% voter turnout
4. 60-day implementation window

## Article V: Transparency

The Foundation commits to:
- Monthly financial reports
- Quarterly ecosystem progress updates
- Public meeting minutes
- Open-source everything
