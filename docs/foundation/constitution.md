# Udaya Governance Constitution

## Article I: Core Principles

### 1.1 Sovereignty of Proof-of-Work
Udaya is a permissionless Proof-of-Work blockchain. No entity may alter the monetary policy (21M cap, 50 UDYA initial reward, 210K halving interval) without overwhelming global consensus.

### 1.2 Decentralization Mandate
The network shall resist centralization. Mining, node operation, development, and governance shall remain accessible to all participants globally.

### 1.3 Censorship Resistance
No transaction that follows consensus rules shall be censored. No address shall be blacklisted at the protocol level.

### 1.4 Open Source
All protocol software shall remain open-source under the MIT License. No proprietary extensions may be required for network participation.

## Article II: Udaya Improvement Proposals (BFIPs)

### 2.1 BFIP Process
1. **Draft** — Any community member may submit a BFIP via GitHub
2. **Review** — Technical Steering Committee reviews for feasibility (14 days)
3. **Feedback** — 30-day public comment period
4. **Vote** — On-chain signaling vote (14 days)
5. **Activation** — If passed, miner activation via BIP-9 style version bits

### 2.2 BFIP Categories
- **Standard**: Protocol changes affecting consensus
- **Informational**: Design guidelines, best practices
- **Process**: Governance and operational changes

### 2.3 Activation Thresholds
- Soft Fork: 95% miner signaling over 2016-block window
- Hard Fork: 85% miner signaling + 60% community vote
- Parameter Change: 60% community vote + 10% quorum

## Article III: Treasury Governance

### 3.1 Treasury Proposals
Proposals for treasury spending must include:
- Detailed budget and milestones
- Deliverable specifications
- Team credentials
- Review period: 14 days minimum

### 3.2 Approval Requirements
- < 10,000 UDYA: Council majority approval
- 10,000–100,000 UDYA: Council supermajority (5/7)
- > 100,000 UDYA: On-chain community vote

## Article IV: Code of Conduct

All participants agree to:
- Act in good faith for the network's benefit
- Disclose conflicts of interest
- Follow responsible disclosure for security issues
- Respect all community members regardless of background

## Article V: Dispute Resolution

Disputes shall be resolved through:
1. Informal mediation by Technical Steering Committee
2. Formal arbitration by Foundation Council
3. Network-level fork if irreconcilable differences exist
