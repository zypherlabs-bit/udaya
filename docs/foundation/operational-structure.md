# Udaya Foundation Operational Structure

## Council Members (Elected)
| Role | Term | Elected By |
|------|------|-----------|
| Chairperson | 2 years | Foundation Council |
| Vice Chair | 2 years | Foundation Council |
| Treasurer | 2 years | Foundation Council |
| Secretary | 2 years | Foundation Council |
| Community Representative | 2 years | UDYA Holders |
| Technical Representative | 2 years | Core Devs |
| Mining Representative | 2 years | Miners |

## Committees
1. **Technical Steering Committee** — Core developers, maintainers, senior contributors
2. **Treasury Committee** — 3 members overseeing financial operations
3. **Community Advisory Board** — 5 community-elected representatives
4. **Security Response Team** — 3 security researchers for incident response

## Operational Cadence
- **Council Meetings**: Weekly (public)
- **Committee Meetings**: Bi-weekly
- **Community Calls**: Monthly
- **Transparency Reports**: Quarterly
- **Foundation Elections**: Annual

## Budget & Compensation
- Council members: Unpaid (volunteer role)
- Core developers: Funded through grants
- Security researchers: Bug bounties + retainers
- Operations: Administrative expenses from treasury
