## 📥 Submission

### How to Submit

1. **Save this form**: Fill out the template and save as a text file
2. **Submit via GitHub**: Open an issue with your feedback
3. **Submit via Email**: Send to [feedback@udaya.org](mailto:feedback@udaya.org)
4. **Submit via Discord**: Share in our #feedback channel

### What Happens Next

- Your feedback will be reviewed by our team
- We may follow up for clarification or details
- Your input will directly influence our roadmap
- We'll acknowledge your contribution (if you include contact info)

## 📝 Feedback Guidelines

### Tips for Effective Feedback

✅ **Be specific**: Provide concrete examples
✅ **Be constructive**: Focus on actionable suggestions
✅ **Be honest**: Share both positives and negatives
✅ **Be detailed**: More context helps us understand
✅ **Be respectful**: Professional tone is appreciated

### What We're Looking For

🔍 **Detailed experiences**: What worked, what didn't
🔍 **Comparative insights**: How Udaya stacks up
🔍 **Creative ideas**: Innovative suggestions
🔍 **Honest assessments**: Both praise and criticism
🔍 **Real-world use cases**: How you'd use Udaya
