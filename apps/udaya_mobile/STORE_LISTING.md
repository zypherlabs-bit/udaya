### Full Description

Udaya Mobile is the official native client for the Udaya Layer-1 blockchain. Manage your UDAYA tokens, explore the blockchain, run a full node, and participate in mining—all from your Android device.

Key Features:
- Secure HD wallet with BIP39/BIP84 support
- Send and receive UDAYA with QR code scanning
- Real-time balance and transaction history
- Full node connectivity and peer management
- Built-in mining dashboard
- Blockchain explorer with block and transaction search
- Network monitoring and diagnostics
- Biometric authentication and passphrase protection
- Support for mainnet, testnet, and regtest networks
- Dark and light theme support

Udaya is a proof-of-work Layer-1 blockchain focused on decentralization, security, and scalability. The mobile client connects to Udaya nodes via JSON-RPC, giving you full control over your funds and participation in the network.
